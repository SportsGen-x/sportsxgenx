Academy Features

Everything Your Academy Needs to Go Digital

From the first membership to tournament results and performance analytics — SportsGenX manages every aspect of academy operations.

Dedicated Academy Profile

A fully branded public profile for your academy on the SportsGenX platform — visible to players, parents, and tournament organisers in your city and sport.

Membership Management

Create membership plans, track renewals, collect fees online, and manage active vs expired memberships — all from one dashboard. Auto-reminders for renewals sent to players.

Player Performance Tracking

Track individual player statistics across every tournament — matches played, wins, losses, points earned, and ranking movement over time. Share performance reports with players and parents.

Tournament Participation History

A complete record of every tournament every player has participated in — categories, results, certificates earned. Searchable and downloadable.

Notifications & Communication

Send push notifications and WhatsApp messages to all members, specific groups, or individual players. Tournament announcements, match schedules, renewal reminders — all built in.

Revenue Reports

Full financial visibility — membership fee collection, tournament entry revenues, outstanding payments, and monthly/annual summaries. Export to PDF or share with your finance team.

Tournament Management

Run your own academy tournaments directly from the platform — registration, draws, scoring, and e-certificates. Or list your players for external tournaments.

Attendance Tracking

Log and monitor player attendance at training sessions and events. Identify consistently absent players and follow up proactively to retain members.

Player–Coach Connect

Connect players with the right coaches from your academy roster. Players can view coach profiles, specializations, and request sessions — all within the SportsGenX ecosystem.
