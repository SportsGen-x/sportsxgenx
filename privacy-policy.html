SportsGenX ("SportsGenX", "we", "our", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you use the SportsGenX website, mobile application, and related services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you use the Platform:

  • Personal details such as your name, email address, and phone number.
  • Account and profile information you provide during registration.
  • Sports participation data, match results, and player rankings.
  • Payment data processed via secure third-party payment gateways.
  • Device information and usage data collected automatically when you access the Platform.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used to:

  • Operate and manage the SportsGenX platform and its features.
  • Organise tournaments and events on behalf of sports organisers.
  • Process payments and manage subscriptions.
  • Improve platform performance, features, and user experience.
  • Send you relevant communications, updates, and notifications.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information only with:

  • Tournament organisers — limited to event-related data required to manage your participation.
  • Trusted service providers — third parties who assist in operating the Platform under strict confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal authorities — when required by law, court order, or governmental regulation.

4. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience on the Platform, remember your preferences, and analyse how the Platform is used. You may disable cookies through your browser settings; however, some features of the Platform may not function correctly without them.

5. Data Security

We apply reasonable and industry-standard security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no system or method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access & correction — you may access and update your personal information through your account settings.
  • Account deletion — you may request the deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.
  • Opt-out — you may opt out of marketing communications at any time.

7. Children's Privacy

SportsGenX does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 without verifiable parental or guardian consent. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information without consent, please contact us and we will take steps to remove the information.

8. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. We will notify users of significant changes where practicable. Continued use of the Platform after any update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
