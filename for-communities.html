Community Features

Everything You Need to Run a Thriving Community

From member onboarding to exclusive tournaments and engagement tools — SportsGenX gives sports communities a complete management platform.

📝

Member Registration

Online member registration with customizable intake forms. Members register via app or web, submit required details, and join your community in minutes.

✅

Verification Process

Ensure community quality with a member verification workflow. Review applications, request documents, and approve members before granting access — keeping your community exclusive.

💳

Membership Payments

Collect monthly, quarterly, or annual membership fees online — UPI, cards, and net banking. Auto-renewal reminders, payment tracking, and instant access grants on payment confirmation.

🏆

Exclusive Tournaments

Run members-only tournaments accessible only to verified community members. Non-members see the event but cannot register — protecting the exclusivity of your community events.

📣

Community Notifications

Send push notifications and WhatsApp messages to the entire community or specific segments. Event announcements, match results, membership reminders, and community updates.

🤝

Member Engagement

Keep members active with leaderboards, rankings, tournament history, and performance tracking. Engaged members renew memberships and bring new players to your community.

📊

Community Analytics

Track membership growth, renewal rates, tournament participation, and engagement levels. Understand what's working and where to focus your community-building efforts.

🏅

Community Rankings

Maintain a community-specific leaderboard — rankings that only include your members. A powerful engagement tool that motivates players to participate in more community events.

🎓

Digital Certificates

Issue branded community certificates for tournament winners, participants, and membership milestones — creating a sense of belonging and achievement for every member.
