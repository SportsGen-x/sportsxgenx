SportsGenX is a comprehensive sports tournament management software designed for organisers, sporting associations, academies, and players across India.

Whether you're running a local badminton knockout in Indore, a premier cricket league in Mumbai, or an ITF-level tennis tournament — SportsGenX manages every step: online player registration, secure entry fee collection, automated draw and fixture generation, live scoring, player auction management, and digital e-certificate distribution.

Beyond tournaments, the platform serves as a complete sports ecosystem — listing sports venues for slot booking, connecting players with academies and coaches, and tracking player performance analytics and city leaderboards.

Our mission: to make every sports event — from grassroots to national level — run on technology, not on WhatsApp groups and Excel sheets.

Platform Capabilities

Exclusive Features

Everything a tournament organiser needs — from the first registration to the final e-certificate — in one powerful platform.

Tournament Publishing

Publish Your Tournament to Thousands — Instantly

List your sports event on the SportsGenX platform and reach thousands of players across your city and sport. Your tournament goes live in under 60 seconds — no technical setup needed.

Online Participation

Sports Tournament Registration App — Zero Manual Work

Players register directly from the app or via WhatsApp — entries auto-populate, duplicate detection runs automatically, and you get a real-time registered-player dashboard.

Fee Management

Online Entry Fee Collection & Payment Management

Collect tournament entry fees online — UPI, cards, and net banking. Every payment is tracked, reconciled, and settled automatically. No more chasing screenshots. Full audit trail and next-day settlement.

Draws & Brackets

Tournament Fixture Generator & Bracket Maker — Automated

Auto-creates brackets, draws, and match schedules for any format — knockout, round robin, league, Premier League, or lucky draw. The bracket maker builds the complete fixture in seconds. Fully automated. Zero errors.

Live Scoring

Live Sports Scoring App with Real-Time Display & Streaming

Match scores update in real time — visible to players, spectators, and organisers instantly on-screen and in-app. Umpires score directly from their phone. Venue displays sync automatically. Live streaming integration included.

E-Certificates

Automated Sports E-Certificates for Every Tournament

Instantly generate and distribute personalised digital sports certificates to winners, runners-up, and all participants — directly from the SportsGenX platform.

Player Auction

Player Auction Software for Leagues — Real-Time Bidding

Live, real-time player bidding for cricket leagues, badminton leagues, and any team-format tournament. Bids update instantly on the full-screen venue display. Team budgets, player base prices, and unsold players tracked automatically.

Premier League Management

Sports League Management Software — Points Tables, Groups & Pools

Automated points tables, pool/group stage scheduling, head-to-head records, and playoff qualification tracking. Supports premier leagues, knockout leagues, and hybrid formats across any sport.
